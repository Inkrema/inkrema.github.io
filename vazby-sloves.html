4. Sloveso + osoba + infinitiv bez to

letnechat, dovolitMy mum let me use her car.
makepřimět, donutitThe film made me cry.
helppomoct (s to i bez)Can you help me (to) carry this?
V trpném rodě se to u make vrací: We were made to wait. Help jde i bez osoby: This will help (to) save money.

5. Obojí, význam stejný nebo skoro stejný

begin, start, continue: It started to rain. = It started raining.
like, love, hate, prefer: rozdíl je malý, -ing spíš o prožitku, to spíš o zvyku: I love swimming. · I like to swim before breakfast. Jdou i s osobou: I like my friends to be on time.
Ale would like / love / hate / prefer mají jen to: I'd love to come.

7. Uvozovací slovesa v nepřímé řeči

vazbaslovesapříklad
+ toagree, offer, promise, refuse, threatenShe promised to help me.
+ osoba + toadvise, ask, encourage, invite, order, persuade, remind, tell, warnHe reminded me to bring my passport.
+ -ingadmit (to), deny, recommend, suggestHe denied taking the money.
+ předložka + -ingapologise for, insist onShe apologised for being late.
+ osoba + předl. + -ingaccuse sb of, blame sb for, congratulate sb on, thank sb forThey accused him of lying.
+ (that)say (to sb), tell sbShe told me (that) she was tired.
+ thatexplain (to sb), complain (to sb), insistShe explained to me that the bus was late.
Pozor: explained to me, ne explained me · accused him of, ne for · apologised for being late, ne apologised to be late.
